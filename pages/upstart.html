Frequently Asked Questions About Upstart

What credit score do you need for Upstart?

Upstart has no minimum credit score requirement. Its AI model evaluates over 1,600 data points including education and employment history. Borrowers with scores as low as 300 have been approved, though better scores typically result in lower APRs.

What is Upstart's debt-to-income requirement?

Upstart requires a debt-to-income ratio (DTI) below 45%–50%. Calculate your DTI by dividing your total monthly debt payments by your gross monthly income. A lower DTI improves your approval odds and typically results in a lower APR.

Does Upstart charge an origination fee?

Yes. Upstart charges an origination fee of 0%–12% of the loan amount. This fee is deducted from your loan proceeds before disbursement and is included in your APR calculation. It is fully disclosed before you accept any offer.

How fast does Upstart fund loans?

Upstart typically funds loans within 1 business day of acceptance. In some cases, funds can arrive the same day. Loan proceeds are deposited directly to your bank account.

Does prequalification hurt my credit score?

No. Upstart's prequalification uses a soft credit pull that has zero impact on your credit score. A hard inquiry only occurs when you formally accept a loan offer.

Is Upstart good for bad credit?

Yes. Upstart is one of the best options for borrowers with bad or limited credit because its AI model looks beyond the FICO score. It considers education, employment history, and area of study, allowing it to approve borrowers that traditional lenders would decline.
