Avant vs Upgrade (2026)

Two lenders built for below-average credit — but they're not interchangeable. Avant reaches lower scores and funds fast; Upgrade lends more and adds credit tools. Here's which fits your situation.

Our take in 10 seconds
Avant is the better bet if your score is in the 500s to low 600s — it approves lower credit than most and funds as soon as the next business day. Upgrade edges ahead once you're around 600+, want a larger loan (up to $50K), or value its built-in credit-monitoring tools. Both charge a fee, so compare the APR, not the rate. And because both target fair credit, it's worth checking Upstart alongside them — its no-minimum-score model often prices thin files better than either.

If you're comparing Avant and Upgrade, you almost certainly have fair or rebuilding credit — that's the exact borrower both lenders court. The good news is you have real options at this credit level. The bad news is both charge fees and can price you near the top of their range, so the difference between them comes down to how low your score is, how much you need, and whether you want credit-building extras.

When Avant is the better choice

Avant's main advantage is reach: it approves borrowers with scores in the high 500s more readily than most mainstream lenders, and it's genuinely fast, often funding the next business day. If you've been declined elsewhere or need money quickly for a smaller amount, Avant is a realistic yes. The trade-offs: it caps loans at $35,000, charges an administration fee, and doesn't allow co-borrowers, so you can't lean on a partner's stronger credit to bring your rate down.

When Upgrade is the better choice

Upgrade suits you once your score is around 600 or above, or when you need more than Avant will lend. It goes up to $50,000, allows joint applications (a co-borrower can lower your rate), and bundles in free credit-monitoring and score tools that are genuinely useful while you're rebuilding. Its origination fee runs 1.85%–9.99%, so like Avant, your effective cost is higher than the headline rate — always read the APR.

Frequently asked questions

Is Avant or Upgrade easier to get approved for?
Avant typically approves lower credit scores — often into the high 500s — while Upgrade usually wants around 600 or above. If your score is in the 500s, Avant (or Upstart, which has no minimum) is the more realistic option.
Do both Avant and Upgrade charge fees?
Yes. Avant charges an administration fee (up to about 4.75%) and Upgrade charges an origination fee of 1.85% to 9.99%. Both are deducted from your loan, so compare the APR rather than the interest rate.
Which lends more, Avant or Upgrade?
Upgrade lends up to $50,000 versus Avant's $35,000 cap. If you need a larger loan, Upgrade (or Upstart, up to $50,000) is the better fit, assuming you qualify.
Can I get either loan with bad credit?
Avant is one of the more accessible mainstream lenders for scores around 580. If you're below that, focus on soft-pull pre-qualification and consider building your score first — see our bad-credit loan guide.
