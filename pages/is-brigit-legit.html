Company Overview

Brigit (Bridge It, Inc.) was founded in 2017 with the mission of helping Americans build a brighter financial future. The app provides cash advances up to $250, credit builder services, and budgeting tools without requiring a credit check. While it boasts millions of users and offers a compelling alternative to payday loans by not charging interest or late fees, the company has faced significant regulatory scrutiny. In November 2023, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) took action against Brigit, resulting in an $18 million settlement for allegedly deceiving consumers with false promises of "instant" cash advances and employing a confusing cancellation process [1].

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • No credit check required for cash advances
  • No interest charges or late fees
  • Includes budgeting and credit-building tools
  • Strong security measures (256-bit encryption) [2]

Cons

  • Monthly subscription fee ($8.99 to $14.99) required for cash advances
  • Maximum advance is relatively low ($250)
  • Recent FTC action for deceptive practices and cancellation difficulties [1]
  • No phone-based customer support

Who Is It Best For

Brigit is best suited for individuals who frequently face minor cash shortfalls before payday and want to avoid costly bank overdraft fees. It is also a viable option for those with poor or no credit history, as the app does not perform a hard credit pull. However, users must be comfortable paying a monthly subscription fee and should carefully monitor their usage to ensure the benefits outweigh the costs. It is particularly useful for those who can consistently repay advances on time to leverage the budgeting and credit-building features effectively.

Red Flags to Watch

The most significant red flag for Brigit is its recent history with the FTC. The agency alleged that Brigit used "dark patterns" to make it difficult for users to cancel their subscriptions and failed to deliver on promises of instant cash advances without hidden fees. While the company has settled these claims and presumably updated its practices, users should remain vigilant when signing up and ensure they understand the cancellation process. Always read the terms of service carefully and be aware of how to manage or cancel your subscription [1].

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Brigit a safe app to use?
Yes, from a technical standpoint, Brigit is safe. It uses 256-bit encryption, multi-factor authentication, and bank-level security to protect your financial data [2]. However, users should be aware of the recent FTC settlement regarding its business practices and exercise caution [1].
2. Does Brigit check your credit?
No, Brigit does not perform a hard or soft credit check when you apply for a cash advance. Instead, it analyzes your bank account activity and income to determine your eligibility, making it accessible to those with limited credit history.
3. How much does Brigit cost?
Brigit offers a free basic plan with budgeting tools, but to access cash advances, you must subscribe to a paid plan, which ranges from $8.99 to $14.99 per month. There are no interest charges or late fees on the advances themselves, but the subscription fee is mandatory for the advance feature.
4. Why was Brigit sued by the FTC?
In late 2023, the FTC took action against Brigit for allegedly deceiving consumers about "instant" cash advances, charging hidden fees for quick delivery, and making it intentionally difficult to cancel the monthly subscription. The company agreed to an $18 million settlement to provide refunds to affected users [1].
