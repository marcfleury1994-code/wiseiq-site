Insider Knowledge Most Guides Don't Cover

💡

The Statement Closing Date Secret

Your credit report shows your balance on your statement closing date, not the day you pay. Paying before the closing date — not the due date — can add 20–50 points in 30 days.

⚖️

Why "Verified" Doesn't Mean Correct

When a dispute comes back "verified," most people give up. Verification often just means the creditor confirmed the item without reviewing your documentation. The kit explains your CFPB complaint and FCRA lawsuit options.

🎯

The 30-10-1 Utilization Rule

Everyone says keep utilization below 30%. The real target is below 10% per card — and keeping at least one card at 1% (not 0%) is better than zero. This distinction is rarely explained.

🚫

Never Pay a Collection Without This

Paying a collection does not remove it from your report — it just changes the status. The kit explains how to negotiate a pay-for-delete agreement before paying, and what to do if the collector refuses.
