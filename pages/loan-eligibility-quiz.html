Frequently Asked Questions

Does this quiz affect my credit score? +
No. This quiz does not perform a hard credit inquiry. It uses your self-reported credit score range and loan purpose to match you with lenders. Any soft pull or pre-qualification check done by lenders after you click through also does not affect your credit score.
How accurate are the quiz results? +
The quiz matches you to lenders based on their published eligibility criteria for your credit score range and loan purpose. Actual approval depends on your full credit profile, income, and debt-to-income ratio — but the quiz gives you a strong starting point to avoid applying with lenders who are unlikely to approve you.
What credit score do I need for a personal loan? +
It depends on the lender. Upstart has no minimum credit score requirement. Avant requires 580+. LendingClub requires 600+. SoFi and LightStream require 660+. The quiz will show you which lenders match your specific score range.
