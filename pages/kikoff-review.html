Our take in 10 seconds
Kikoff can help you build credit if you're new to credit or your file is thin. For $5 a month you get a $750 Kikoff tradeline whose on-time payments are reported to the major credit bureaus, and there's no hard credit check to open it. If you have little or no credit history, most of the usual advice — pay down cards, keep old accounts open — doesn't apply, because you don't have accounts to work with yet. That's the specific problem products like Kikoff are designed for: giving you a positive tradeline to establish a credit history from scratch. This review covers what Kikoff actually is, how it works, what it costs, and honestly who should and shouldn't bother.

What is Kikoff?

Kikoff is a credit-building service, not a loan or a credit card. When you sign up for the basic plan (about $5 per month), you get a $750 Kikoff Credit Account — a tradeline you can use toward items in Kikoff's own store. You pay it back in small monthly amounts, and Kikoff reports your payment history to the major credit bureaus. There's no hard credit check to open an account, and the basic plan charges no interest and no hidden fees beyond the monthly cost.

How Kikoff works, step by step

  • Sign up. Opening a Kikoff account takes a few minutes and does not require a hard credit check.
  • Get your $750 tradeline. You receive a $750 Kikoff Credit Account you can use in the Kikoff store.
  • Make small monthly payments. You pay down what you use in low monthly amounts you choose.
  • Kikoff reports your payments. Your on-time payment history is reported to the major credit bureaus, which is how a positive tradeline helps you build credit over time.
The mechanism that matters: payment history is the largest factor in building credit. A tradeline like Kikoff's works by adding consistent, on-time payments to your file. That only helps if you actually pay on time every month — a single missed payment works against you, so treat it like any other bill.

What Kikoff can and can't do

Being honest about the limits is the whole point of a review. Here's the straight version:

What it can do

  • Help you build credit history from scratch
  • Add a positive, on-time tradeline to your file
  • Do it cheaply ($5/mo) with no hard pull
  • Work for people banks usually turn away

What it can't do

  • Remove negative marks already on your file
  • Replace paying down high balances elsewhere
  • Act as a loan, credit card, or source of cash
  • Do anything if you stop paying on time

In short, Kikoff is a tool for building credit, not for undoing past problems. If your file is thin or empty, it addresses your exact situation. If your challenge is derogatory marks or high balances on existing accounts, a tradeline alone won't move the needle — you'd want to pair it with the steps in our credit-building hub.

Who Kikoff is best for

  • People new to credit — students, young adults, or anyone with a thin or empty file who needs to establish history.
  • People rebuilding who want an inexpensive, low-effort positive tradeline while they work on the rest.
  • Anyone who wants to avoid a hard credit check just to get started.

It's less useful if you already have a healthy, established credit file, or if your main issue is negative items or high utilization — in those cases your money and attention are better spent elsewhere first.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kikoff help build credit?
Yes. Kikoff reports your on-time payments on a $750 tradeline to the major credit bureaus, which is how a positive tradeline helps you build credit over time. Results depend on paying on time and on the rest of your credit profile.
How much does Kikoff cost?
Kikoff's basic credit-building plan is about $5 per month. It has no hard credit check to sign up and, on the basic plan, no interest or hidden fees beyond the monthly cost.
Is Kikoff a loan or a credit card?
No. Kikoff is not a loan, credit card, credit line, or cash. When you sign up you get a $750 Kikoff tradeline (a Credit Account) you use in the Kikoff store and pay back in small monthly amounts.
Does signing up for Kikoff require a credit check?
There is no hard credit check to open a Kikoff account, which is part of why it's accessible to people who are new to credit or have a thin file.
Who should use Kikoff?
It's best for people who are new to credit or building it and want an inexpensive, positive tradeline. It's less useful for people with an established file or those whose main issue is negative marks or high balances.
