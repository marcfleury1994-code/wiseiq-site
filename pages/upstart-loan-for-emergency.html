Eligibility and Requirements for an Upstart Emergency Loan

While Upstart's AI underwriting expands access to credit, there are still fundamental eligibility criteria you'll need to meet to qualify for an Upstart emergency loan. Understanding these requirements beforehand can help streamline your application process and increase your chances of approval for urgent personal loan needs.

  • Age: You must be at least 18 years old (19 in Alabama and Nebraska).
  • Residency: You must be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident, and have a U.S. physical address.
  • Income: You need a verifiable source of income. This doesn't necessarily mean traditional employment; Upstart considers various income streams.
  • Bank Account: You must have a U.S. bank account to receive funds and make payments.
  • Email Address: A valid email address is required for communication.
  • Minimum FICO Score: While Upstart emphasizes AI underwriting, they do have a minimum FICO score requirement, typically around 600. However, applicants with no FICO score may still be approved if they meet other criteria.
  • Debt-to-Income Ratio: Your debt-to-income ratio (DTI) will be assessed to ensure you can comfortably manage new monthly payments. A lower DTI generally improves your approval odds.
  • Education and Employment: Upstart places significant weight on your educational background and employment history, viewing these as indicators of future financial stability.

It's important to remember that Upstart performs a soft credit pull during prequalification, which won't affect your credit score. However, if you proceed with a loan offer, a hard credit inquiry will be made, which may have a temporary, minor impact on your FICO score. This is a standard practice for most lenders when finalizing a loan.
