Our Editorial Process

Every piece of content published on WiseIQ goes through a structured editorial process designed to ensure accuracy, clarity, and genuine usefulness:

1

Primary Source Research

We begin with primary sources: CFPB publications, Federal Reserve reports, FICO documentation, and official credit bureau consumer guides. We do not rely on secondary aggregators or other personal finance sites as primary sources.

2

Drafting & Fact-Checking

Content is drafted with specific citations and data points. All statistics, credit score ranges, interest rate figures, and regulatory claims are verified against the original source before publication.

3

Accuracy Review

A second team member reviews all factual claims, confirms affiliate disclosures are present where required, and verifies that the content does not make unsubstantiated promises or guarantees.

4

Regular Updates

Financial regulations, credit scoring models, and product terms change. We review all published content at least annually and update immediately when we become aware of significant changes that affect accuracy.
