Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main difference between Best Egg and Upstart?
The primary difference lies in their underwriting models. Best Egg relies more on traditional credit scores (FICO) and credit history, while Upstart uses an AI-driven approach that considers factors like education, employment, and income in addition to credit score, making it more accessible to those with limited credit history.
Which lender offers lower interest rates?
Both lenders offer competitive rates, but Upstart has a slightly lower minimum APR (6.2%) compared to Best Egg (6.99%). However, the actual rate you receive depends on your creditworthiness and other factors, and borrowers with excellent credit might find similar rates from both.
Can I get a loan from Best Egg or Upstart with bad credit?
Best Egg generally requires a minimum credit score of 640, so it's not ideal for those with bad credit. Upstart, on the other hand, is more accommodating to borrowers with limited or lower credit scores due to its AI-powered underwriting, which looks beyond just FICO scores.
Do Best Egg and Upstart charge origination fees?
Yes, both lenders charge origination fees. Best Egg's origination fees range from 0.99% to 9.99%, while Upstart's can range from 0% to 12%. These fees are typically deducted from the loan proceeds before you receive the funds.

💡 Expert Tip: Check Your Rate Before You Commit

Upstart offers prequalification with a soft credit pull — meaning you can check your rate in minutes without any impact to your credit score. Before applying, review your debt-to-income ratio (DTI): Upstart looks for a DTI below 45–50%. Divide your total monthly debt payments by your gross monthly income to calculate yours. A lower DTI improves your approval odds and typically results in a lower APR.
