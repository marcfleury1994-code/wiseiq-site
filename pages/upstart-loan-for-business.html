Eligibility and Requirements for an Upstart Business Startup Loan

While Upstart's lending model is more inclusive than many traditional lenders, there are still specific eligibility criteria you'll need to meet to secure an Upstart loan for business startup. These requirements ensure that borrowers have a reasonable capacity to repay the loan.

  • Credit Score: While Upstart considers non-traditional credit factors, a minimum FICO score is generally required, often around 600. However, applicants with lower scores may still qualify if other factors are strong.
  • Debt-to-Income Ratio: Your debt-to-income ratio (DTI) is a crucial factor. Upstart assesses your ability to manage additional debt based on your current income versus existing debt obligations. A lower DTI is generally more favorable.
  • Education and Employment: Upstart places significant emphasis on your educational background and employment history. A college degree and a stable job can significantly improve your chances of approval, even if your credit history is thin.
  • Income: You must have a verifiable source of income. This is essential for demonstrating your ability to make consistent monthly payments. For self-employed borrowers, this might involve providing tax returns or bank statements to prove income stability.
  • Residency: You must be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident and at least 18 years old.

It's important to remember that even if you meet these criteria, approval is not guaranteed. Upstart's AI underwriting system performs a comprehensive evaluation. The initial soft credit pull during prequalification will give you a good indication of your eligibility without affecting your credit report.
