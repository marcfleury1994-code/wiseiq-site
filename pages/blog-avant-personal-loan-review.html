Avant has established itself as a prominent online lender, particularly catering to individuals with fair to good credit scores. Since its inception, Avant has aimed to provide accessible and transparent personal loan options, distinguishing itself through a streamlined application process and rapid funding capabilities. This review delves into the specifics of Avant's offerings, helping potential borrowers understand if it's the right fit for their financial needs.

Who is Avant Best For?

Avant personal loans are particularly well-suited for individuals with fair credit scores, typically ranging from 580 to 700. If you've had some credit challenges in the past but are looking for a legitimate lending option, Avant could be a strong contender. Their quick funding process, often delivering funds by the next business day, makes them an excellent choice for those facing urgent financial needs, such as unexpected medical bills, car repairs, or consolidating high-interest debt. The ability to check your rate without impacting your credit score is also a significant advantage, allowing borrowers to explore their options risk-free. Furthermore, Avant's willingness to consider applicants with less-than-perfect credit sets it apart from traditional banks, which often have stricter lending criteria. This inclusivity makes Avant a vital resource for a segment of the population that might otherwise struggle to secure financing.

How Avant Personal Loans Work

Avant simplifies the personal loan application process, making it straightforward for borrowers to apply online. The process begins with a soft credit inquiry when you check your rate, which doesn't affect your credit score. This initial step provides a preliminary offer, giving you an idea of potential loan terms without commitment. If you decide to proceed, you'll complete a full application, which involves a hard credit pull. Avant uses a proprietary underwriting model that considers more than just your credit score, looking at various financial data points to assess your creditworthiness. This holistic approach can be beneficial for applicants whose credit reports may not fully reflect their financial stability. Once approved, funds are typically disbursed via direct deposit to your bank account as soon as the next business day. Loan terms generally range from 24 to 60 months, providing flexibility in repayment, and payments are typically made on a fixed monthly schedule, making budgeting predictable.

Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for an Avant personal loan, applicants generally need a minimum credit score of 580. Beyond the credit score, Avant considers several other factors, including your income, debt-to-income ratio, and other financial obligations. While there isn't a strict income requirement, applicants should demonstrate a stable income source to show their ability to repay the loan. It's also important to note that Avant loans are not available in all U.S. states, so prospective borrowers should verify availability in their area. Applicants must be at least 18 years old (19 in Alabama) and a U.S. citizen or permanent resident. Having a verifiable bank account is also a prerequisite for receiving funds and making repayments. Avant's eligibility criteria are designed to be accessible while still ensuring responsible lending practices, making it a viable option for many who are overlooked by traditional lenders.

Understanding Avant's Rates and Fees

Avant offers a competitive APR range from 9.95% to 35.99%. The actual rate you receive will depend on your creditworthiness, loan amount, and loan term. It's crucial for borrowers to understand that while the lower end of the APR spectrum is attractive, those with lower credit scores will likely be offered rates closer to the higher end. Avant also charges an administration fee of up to 4.75%, which is deducted from the loan proceeds before disbursement. This fee is a one-time charge and is common among online lenders. Borrowers should factor this fee into their total loan cost when comparing offers. There are no prepayment penalties, allowing borrowers to save on interest by paying off their loan early.

Customer Service and Reputation

Avant maintains a strong online presence and generally receives positive customer reviews, particularly regarding its straightforward application process and quick funding. The company offers customer support via phone and email, with representatives available to assist with applications and loan management. Avant is accredited by the Better Business Bureau (BBB) and holds an A- rating, indicating a commitment to resolving customer complaints. While some customers report high interest rates, this is often a reflection of their credit profile rather than a criticism of Avant's service. Overall, Avant is considered a reputable lender in the online personal loan space.

Who Should Look Elsewhere

A personal loan is not the right tool for every situation. Consider alternatives if any of the following apply to you:

  • You have home equity: A HELOC typically offers rates 5–10% lower than personal loans. If you own your home, compare HELOC rates before taking a personal loan.
  • Your debt is primarily credit card debt: A balance transfer card with a 0% intro APR (typically 12–21 months) will cost less than a personal loan if you can pay off the balance within the intro period.
  • You need less than $1,000: Most personal loan lenders have minimum amounts of $1,000–$2,000. For smaller needs, a credit union payday alternative loan (PAL) or a 0% APR credit card may be more appropriate.
  • Your credit score is below 500: Most personal loan lenders — including those that accept "bad credit" — have practical minimums around 500–560. Below this, secured loans, credit-builder loans, or co-signer arrangements are more realistic options.
  • You are in active bankruptcy: Personal loan lenders will decline applicants in active Chapter 7 or Chapter 13 proceedings. Resolve your bankruptcy first.

Focus on the Annual Percentage Rate (APR), which includes both interest and fees. Compare minimum credit score requirements, funding speed, loan amounts, and repayment terms. Read recent customer reviews on Trustpilot and the BBB. Getting pre-qualified lets you see real personalized offers.

The interest rate is the base cost of borrowing. APR (Annual Percentage Rate) includes the interest rate plus all fees (origination fees, closing costs, etc.), expressed as a yearly rate. APR gives you a more complete picture of the true cost of a loan — always compare APRs, not just interest rates.

Credit scores have a dramatic impact on rates. On a $20,000 personal loan, the difference between a 720 score (8% APR) and a 580 score (25% APR) is over $9,000 in additional interest over 5 years. Improving your score before applying can save thousands.

Reputable online lenders use bank-level encryption (256-bit SSL) to protect your data. Look for HTTPS in the URL, check that the lender is registered in your state, verify their BBB rating, and read privacy policies before submitting personal information. Avoid lenders who contact you unsolicited.
