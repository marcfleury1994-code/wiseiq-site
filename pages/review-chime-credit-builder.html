The Chime Credit Builder Secured Visa® is one of the most accessible credit-building tools available. Unlike most secured cards that require a $200+ security deposit, Chime has no minimum deposit requirement — you move whatever amount you want from your Chime Checking Account into the Credit Builder secured account, and that becomes your spending limit.

WiseIQ Expert Tip

Financial decisions made with complete information consistently outperform those made under pressure or with incomplete data. Take time to compare at least 3 options before committing.

There's no annual fee, no interest charges (since you're spending your own money), and no credit check. The card reports to all three major credit bureaus each month. Chime's Safer Credit Building feature automatically pays your full balance each month, eliminating the risk of missed payments.
