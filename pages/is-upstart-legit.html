Common Concerns — Addressed

Is the origination fee a scam? No. The origination fee (0%–12%) is a standard industry practice and is fully disclosed before you accept your loan. It is deducted from your loan proceeds — if you borrow $10,000 with a 5% fee, you receive $9,500. The fee is included in your APR calculation.

Why does Upstart ask for my education history? Upstart's AI model uses education and employment data as additional signals of creditworthiness. This is disclosed in their privacy policy and is reviewed by the CFPB. It allows Upstart to approve borrowers with limited credit history who would otherwise be declined.

Does prequalification hurt my credit? No. Upstart's prequalification uses a soft credit pull that has zero impact on your credit score. A hard inquiry only occurs if you formally accept a loan offer.
