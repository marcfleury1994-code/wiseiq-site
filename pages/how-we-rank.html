Our Commitment Under CFPB Guidelines

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's 2024 Circular on digital comparison-shopping tools warns that ranking products based on commission rather than consumer benefit can constitute an abusive practice. WiseIQ is designed from the ground up to comply with this standard. Our ranking algorithm weights user benefit factors — approval likelihood, rates, fees, and goal alignment — not commission size. We disclose all affiliate relationships transparently on every page.

The Core Principle

WiseIQ earns a commission when you apply for and are approved for a financial product through our links. This is how we keep the site free. We are transparent about this on every page.

However, commission size does not determine where a product ranks. A product that pays us $200 per approval will not rank above a product that pays $50 if the $50 product is a better fit for your credit profile and financial situation. We believe this is both the right thing to do and the only sustainable business model — users who get approved for the right product come back. Users who get declined because we steered them toward a high-commission product they did not qualify for do not.

Our editorial team reviews all rankings quarterly and maintains independence from our commercial partnerships team.

What We Evaluate

Credit Score Match

We assess the realistic minimum credit score required for approval based on issuer guidelines and publicly reported approval data. Products are only shown as "Strong Match" when your score meets the typical approval threshold.

Rates and Fees

We evaluate the APR range, annual fee, foreign transaction fees, balance transfer fees, and any other costs. Lower-cost products are scored more favorably, all else being equal.

Rewards Value

For rewards cards, we calculate the estimated annual value based on average American spending patterns. We use the same methodology for every card so comparisons are apples-to-apples.

Terms and Conditions

We review the full card agreement including penalty APRs, grace periods, minimum payment requirements, and any terms that could negatively affect cardholders.

Issuer Reputation

We consider J.D. Power customer satisfaction scores, CFPB complaint data, and user reviews when evaluating issuers. A product from an issuer with poor customer service is scored down accordingly.

Goal Alignment

When you complete our quiz, your stated goal (build credit, earn rewards, reduce debt, etc.) influences which products appear at the top of your personalized results. A cash-back card will not rank first for someone whose goal is travel rewards.

Our Review Process — Step by Step

Every product on WiseIQ goes through a structured review process before it is published and on a recurring quarterly cycle thereafter.

Step 1 — Data Collection
We collect the full terms and conditions directly from the issuer's website, including APR ranges, fee schedules, rewards program details, and approval criteria. We do not rely on third-party summaries.
Step 2 — Rubric Scoring
Each product is scored against the five-factor rubric above. Scores are calculated independently by at least one editorial team member and cross-checked for consistency.
Step 3 — Approval Threshold Verification
We verify the realistic minimum credit score for approval using a combination of issuer disclosures, publicly reported approval data from consumer forums, and credit bureau guidance. We err on the conservative side — we would rather show a product as a weaker match than set a user up for a hard inquiry rejection.
Step 4 — Competitive Benchmarking
The product is compared against the top 3–5 alternatives in its category. If a better product exists for the same credit profile and goal, the new product must score meaningfully higher to displace it.
Step 5 — Publication & Dating
The product card is published with a "Last Reviewed" date. This date is updated every time we re-verify the product's terms, not just when something changes.
Step 6 — Quarterly Review Cycle
All products are re-reviewed on a rolling quarterly basis. Products whose terms have materially changed are re-scored immediately. Products that no longer meet our minimum quality threshold are removed from the site.

What "Editor's Pick" Means

Products labeled "Editor's Pick" have been selected by our editorial team as the best overall option in their category based on the criteria above. These designations are reviewed quarterly. A product can lose its Editor's Pick status if its terms change, if better alternatives emerge, or if issuer reputation data deteriorates.

Editor's Pick products may or may not pay higher commissions than non-featured products. The designation is based solely on editorial merit.

Our Affiliate Relationships — Full Disclosure

WiseIQ has affiliate relationships with financial institutions and affiliate networks including FlexOffers. When you click an "Apply Now" button and are approved for a product, WiseIQ typically earns a commission. The commission amount varies by product and institution.

Typical commission ranges (for transparency):

  • Credit cards: $40–$200 per approved application
  • Personal loans: $30–$100 per funded loan or qualified lead
  • Savings accounts: $30–$80 per opened account
  • Investing platforms: $20–$60 per funded account

Not all products on this site have affiliate relationships. Some products are included purely because they are valuable to consumers, even if they generate no revenue for WiseIQ.

How We Keep Editorial Independence

Our editorial team operates independently from our commercial partnerships team. The team that negotiates affiliate agreements does not determine product rankings. Our editorial team has final authority over all rankings, ratings, and recommendations.

We do not accept payment to feature a product, to write a positive review, or to improve a product's ranking. If a financial institution requests changes to our editorial coverage as a condition of an affiliate relationship, we decline.

If you believe a product on our site is inaccurately described or that our rankings are biased, please contact us at wiseiq.official@gmail.com. We take these concerns seriously and investigate all reports.

What We Will Never Do

To make our commitment concrete, here is an explicit list of practices that WiseIQ prohibits regardless of commercial pressure:

  • Accept payment to improve a product's ranking or match score
  • Remove a negative disclosure about a product in exchange for an affiliate relationship
  • Recommend a product we believe a user is unlikely to be approved for in order to generate a commission
  • Use deceptive language such as "pre-approved" when no pre-approval has occurred
  • Show a product as "Highest-Rated Overall — Based on WiseIQ's Methodology" based on commission rate rather than editorial merit
  • Suppress a competitor's product that is objectively better for a user's stated profile

If you believe we have violated any of these commitments, contact wiseiq.official@gmail.com. We investigate all reports and respond within 5 business days.
