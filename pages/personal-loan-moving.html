Should you use a personal loan to move?

A personal loan for moving expenses can be a strategic financial tool, especially when faced with significant upfront costs that exceed your immediate savings. Unlike credit cards, personal loans typically offer lower interest rates and fixed monthly payments, making budgeting more predictable. This can be particularly beneficial for large expenses such as hiring a professional moving company costs or covering a substantial security deposit loan.

How to qualify

Qualifying for a personal loan for moving expenses generally involves meeting certain criteria set by lenders. While requirements vary, common factors include your credit score, debt-to-income ratio, and employment stability. Lenders want to ensure you have the capacity to repay the loan.

For a lender like Upstart, a minimum credit score of 580 is typically required, but their AI underwriting can approve applicants with lower scores if other factors are strong. It's advisable to check your credit score before applying and address any discrepancies. Having a stable income and a manageable debt load will significantly improve your chances of approval for a relocation loan. Some lenders may also require you to be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident and at least 18 years old.

How to reduce moving costs

Even with a personal loan for moving expenses, finding ways to reduce your overall moving costs can save you money and minimize financial strain. Planning ahead is key.

Start by decluttering and selling or donating items you no longer need. The less you move, the less it costs. Get multiple quotes from moving company costs to compare prices and services. Consider moving during the off-peak season (fall or winter) or mid-week, as rates are often lower. Packing yourself with free boxes from local stores can also significantly cut down expenses. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What is a personal loan for moving expenses?
A personal loan for moving expenses is an unsecured installment loan specifically used to cover costs associated with relocating, such as packing, transportation, and new home setup fees. It provides a lump sum that you repay over a fixed period with regular, fixed payments.
Q2: How much can I borrow for moving expenses?
Loan amounts for moving financing typically range from $1,000 to $50,000, depending on the lender and your creditworthiness. The amount you can borrow should align with your estimated moving costs.
Q3: What credit score do I need for a relocation loan?
While requirements vary, many lenders offering relocation loan options look for a minimum credit score of 580 to 660. Lenders like Upstart use alternative data, potentially allowing approval with lower scores if other financial indicators are strong.
Q4: Can I use a personal loan to cover a security deposit?
Yes, a personal loan for moving expenses can absolutely be used to cover a security deposit. This is a common use case, especially when the deposit is substantial and you prefer not to deplete your savings.
Q5: Are there alternatives to a personal loan for moving?
Yes, alternatives include using your savings, borrowing from family or friends, or using credit cards. Each has its own advantages and disadvantages regarding interest rates, repayment terms, and impact on your credit. For other loan purposes, see our guide on personal loans for vacation.
